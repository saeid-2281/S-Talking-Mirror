# S Talking Brand System

## Identity

S Talking is an AI Audio Studio for calm, repeatable production batches. The identity should feel precise, modern, trustworthy, and comfortable for long sessions.

## Logo

The official master logo is `app/resources/brand/official/S-Logo.svg`.
It is the only approved brand source for v0.18 and must not be redrawn,
regenerated, simplified, recolored, or replaced.

Derived app/installer/About assets are generated from this master and tracked
under `app/resources/brand/official/`. See `docs/BRANDING.md` for the SHA256
hash and generated asset list.

Clear space around the symbol should be at least 25% of the symbol width. The minimum symbol size is 16 px; horizontal lockups should not be used below 120 px wide.

Do not stretch the mark, recolor it, add decorative shadows, place it on low-contrast backgrounds, or replace it with a generic microphone or chat icon.

## Colors

Brand Blue is the primary action and focus family. Voice Teal identifies audio/progress. Warm Accent is reserved for warnings, quota attention, and favorites.

Operational colors must use semantic tokens from `app.gui.theme`, not scattered raw hex values.

## Typography

Use system-native fonts: Segoe UI Variable, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif. Avoid bundled proprietary fonts.

Use 22 px semibold for product identity, 18 px semibold for page titles, 14 px semibold for section titles, 13 px for body, 12 px for compact body, and 11 px for metadata.

## Accessibility

Dark and Light themes must keep readable selected rows, visible focus states, and status labels that do not rely on color alone.
